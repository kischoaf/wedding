The beginning of this chapter

From DePaul dorms to forever.

We met in 2018 in the DePaul dorms, Beldine Racine Hall to be exact. We started talking as friends, the way the best things sometimes begin: casually, honestly, without knowing how much a person is about to matter.

Some of the earliest memories still feel perfectly us. Kieran falling asleep on Nang's mattress topper while she was studying for finals. Nang getting her first tattoo and wanting to show him first.

Our first date was the Art Institute of Chicago and sushi at Hot Woks Cool Sushi next to the museum. On the walk there, someone from Kraft was handing out free mac and cheese, which feels like the kind of strange, perfect Chicago detail a story should keep forever. We still have pieces of that day: the museum program, the ticket on Nang's cork board, and the memory of walking through the miniature rooms exhibit telling each other which tiny rooms we wanted to live in.

Years later, we made it official at the Daley Center with a small ceremony and the people closest to us. It was freezing, chaotic, intimate, nostalgic, and genuine. This site is part announcement, part scrapbook, and part update board for everything still ahead.

The ceremony

Watch it with us.

We kept the ceremony private and close, with Mom, Dad, Autumn, and Blake there with us. The day started early at home in our apartment, then turned into the exact kind of story we will tell forever: finding our way alongside a stranger trying to get to marriage court for her daughter, realizing we did not have the ten dollars in cash we needed, borrowing it from Autumn, and exchanging fifteen-dollar Amazon rings.

Afterward, we got Einstein Bros. Bagels and coffee, then returned to the Art Institute and Hot Woks Cool Sushi, carrying the first date back into the wedding day.
